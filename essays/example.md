# A Short Test Piece

Everything is only in your mind. Everything is all in your mind. So why is it
that there is any shared illusion at all? That is the real question, and I have
never once got a straight answer out of anybody about it.

Art is an illusion of separation. Everything is the same material — the same
carbon, the same dirt, the same arrangement of almost nothing — and what art
does is convince us that a piece of it has been lifted out of the whole and
made distinct. A frame is a claim. A plinth is a claim. A line on a page adds
nothing to the page; it only declares a difference, and we all agree to see it.

Which sounds like a trick until you notice that money works the same way, and
borders work the same way, and every institution anybody has ever died for
works the same way. Collective agreement is not a weaker kind of real. It is
most of what real means.
